# Privacy Policy — Synaxis

*Last updated: 13 August 2026*

Synaxis ("the app", "we") is a reader for the lives of the saints and the
Orthodox church calendar. This policy explains, in plain language, what
the app does with your data.

**The short version: nothing leaves your device except the articles,
portraits, and fonts you ask it to fetch.**

## What the app collects

**Nothing that identifies you.** Synaxis has no accounts, no sign-in, no
analytics, no advertising SDKs, no tracking of any kind, and no server of
ours. There is nothing to log in to and nothing to send us.

## What the app stores on your device

- **Your library** — bookmarks, reading history, highlights, and margin
  notes — is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. It
  is never transmitted anywhere.
- **Your settings** — calendar reckoning, palette, fonts, and reading
  preferences — are stored on your device only.
- **Downloaded articles and portraits** are stored in the app's permanent
  private storage so they are available offline.

If you have enabled Android's automatic backup, this local data may be
included in your device backup, as Android provides. You can disable
backup for the app at any time in Android's settings.

## Network access

The only network traffic the app makes is fetching public content you
choose to download:

- **OrthodoxWiki**, **Wikipedia**, and **Wikimedia Commons** — the lives
  of the saints, article text, and portraits, which you can read offline
  afterwards.
- **Google Fonts** — only when you explicitly install a font by name or
  link in the settings screen.

The app identifies itself with a descriptive User-Agent string, as
Wikimedia's API policy requires, and downloads with modest parallelism. It
does not send your location, your contacts, your other apps, or any
personal information.

When you choose "Download now" or "Stream in background," the download
runs as a foreground service so it can finish even if you leave the app;
a progress notification shows its status and you can stop it by
force-stopping the app.

## Permissions

- **Internet** — required to fetch the articles, portraits, and fonts
  described above.
- **Notifications** (Android 13+) — required only to show the download
  progress notification; the app never sends promotional notifications.

## Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children. Its content is religious and biographical in nature.
