## Play Store listing

### Short description (80 chars)

> The lives of the saints, feasts, fasts & a daily word. Offline. Ad-free.

### Full description

Synaxis is a private, ad-free Orthodox reader for Android. It gathers the
commemorations of the day, the lives of the saints, the feasts and fasts of
the church year, and a daily saying from the Fathers — and puts them all on
your phone, beautifully typeset, ready offline.

**Today**
See who is commemorated today, what is being fasted or feasted, and read a
daily saying that follows *your* calendar — Julian (Old Style) or Revised
Julian (New Style). It stays the same all day and turns over at midnight.

**The lives**
Over a thousand verified lives of Eastern Orthodox saints — martyrs,
monks, bishops, fools-for-Christ, elders and new martyrs — browsable by
era, jurisdiction and tag, or through an A–Z list.

**The calendar**
The whole church year on either reckoning, with feasts, fasts and the
saints of every single day — every one of the 366 days is covered.

**The reader**
A reading experience built for long texts: four palettes, your choice of
typeface, size, leading, weight and justification, and drop caps. Select
any passage to highlight it, note it, or copy it. Where both OrthodoxWiki
and Wikipedia have an article, switch between them with a tap.

**Everything offline**
Articles and portraits are fetched at full speed and stored permanently on
your device — not in a wipeable cache. A smart sync at launch tops up only
what's missing, and can run in the background while you read. No account,
no ads, no analytics, no trackers. Your library is yours.

- 🗓 Both calendars — Julian and Revised Julian, switchable in one tap
- 📖 Real articles from OrthodoxWiki and Wikipedia, cleaned and readable
- 🖼 Portraits verified against each saint — no random buildings
- 🕯 A perfect daily saying for every day of the church year
- 🔖 Bookmarks, highlights and margin notes, all stored locally
- 💾 Offline-first: everything you sync stays on the phone
- 🔒 No ads, no accounts, no tracking

## Screenshots to shoot (Play Store / site)

1. **Today** — the header with the day's saints, the daily saying card,
   and the fast/feast card. This is the hero shot.
2. **Calendar** — a month grid with fast levels and feast dots, with the
   Julian / Revised toggle visible.
3. **A saint's life** — the reader with a portrait, drop caps, and clean
   typesetting. Show a famous saint (e.g. St Seraphim of Sarov).
4. **Lives list** — the A–Z index or an era filter, showing the breadth of
   the index.
5. **Reader details** — a highlighted passage with a note, showing
   bookmarks/highlights.
6. **Settings** — palettes and fonts, showing the reading options.

**Framing:** warm light, parchment or night palette depending on the
audience; square 1:1 for social, 9:16 for stories, 1080×1920 for Play
Store feature graphics. Keep the type large — this is a reading app.

## Audience & angle

- **Who:** Orthodox Christians (and inquirers) with a phone; parishioners,
  catechumens, converts, monastics-adjacent readers, the Orthodox internet.
- **The need:** a fast, trustworthy, offline way to follow the church year
  and read the lives — without scrolling a website or paying for a walled
  garden.
- **The difference:**
  - Every day of the year covered on *both* calendars, not a partial set.
  - Saints verified as Eastern Orthodox — no Coptic, no schismatics.
  - Portraits that are actually the person, not a photo of a building.
  - Real articles from both wikis, kept offline, switchable.
  - No account, no ads, no tracking, ever.
